# Refund Policy

**RetailOS Platform**  
Last updated: July 1, 2026

---

## Overview

All RetailOS subscription payments are collected via bank transfer and verified manually by our admin team. Because of this payment model, our refund policy differs from platforms that use automated card processing. Please read this policy carefully before submitting a payment.

---

## 1. Payment Method

RetailOS collects subscription fees exclusively via bank transfer to our designated account. Once you submit a payment through the portal (with your bank, transfer amount, and reference number), our admin team reviews and verifies the transfer, typically within **1–2 business days**.

Your subscription is activated only after successful verification. A rejected or unverified payment does not extend or activate your subscription.

---

## 2. Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds are only considered in the following specific circumstances:

### 2.1 Duplicate Payment
If you accidentally submitted two payments for the same billing cycle (same plan, same period), we will refund the excess amount in full.

**Evidence required:** Both payment reference numbers and bank transaction receipts.

### 2.2 Overpayment
If the transferred amount exceeds the plan price for the selected cycle, the excess amount will be:
- Applied as a credit toward your next billing cycle, **or**
- Refunded to your originating bank account at your request.

### 2.3 Service Unavailability
If the RetailOS platform was demonstrably unavailable (not accessible) for more than **72 consecutive hours** due to a fault on our side (not scheduled maintenance, third-party outages, or force majeure) during an active paid period, you may request a proportional credit or partial refund for the affected period.

**Evidence required:** Date and time range of the outage, with supporting screenshots or logs.

---

## 3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will **not** be issued in the following circumstances:

- **Early cancellation** — You decide to stop using the Service before your current subscription cycle ends.
- **Unused portion of a plan** — You do not use all the features or capacity your plan allows.
- **Account suspension due to ToS violation** — Your account was suspended for breaching our Terms of Service.
- **Change of mind** — You change your mind after a subscription has been verified and activated.
- **Incorrect payment submission** — You submitted the wrong amount, wrong reference number, or transferred to the wrong account due to your own error.
- **Plan downgrade** — Switching to a lower plan does not entitle you to a refund for remaining days on the current plan.
- **Expired requests** — Refund requests submitted more than **30 days** after the payment date will not be considered.

---

## 4. Refund Process

To request a refund, follow these steps:

1. **Email us** at **billing@retailos.pk** with the subject line: `Refund Request – [Your Business Name]`

2. **Include the following in your email:**
   - Business name and registered email address
   - Payment reference number(s)
   - Bank name and account title used for the transfer
   - Clear reason for the refund request
   - Scanned receipt or screenshot of the bank transaction (if available)

3. **Our billing team will review your request** within **3 business days** and respond with an approval or rejection, including a reason if rejected.

4. **Approved refunds** are processed within **5–7 business days** after approval and transferred to your original bank account.

---

## 5. Plan Changes & Upgrades

### Upgrades
Upgrading to a higher plan takes effect from your next billing cycle after the upgrade payment is verified. The remaining days on your current plan are not refunded.

### Downgrades
Switching to a lower plan takes effect from your next billing cycle. No refund is issued for the current cycle's price difference. The lower plan must accommodate your current usage (stores, products, users); if it does not, the downgrade will be blocked until usage is reduced.

---

## 6. Rejected Payments

If your payment is rejected by our admin team (e.g. amount mismatch, unverifiable reference), you will be notified via the portal. A rejected payment:

- Does not activate or extend your subscription.
- Is not automatically refunded (the funds remain in your bank account as the transfer was not completed on our end, or if transferred, a reversal process will be initiated).

Contact **billing@retailos.pk** immediately if you believe a payment was incorrectly rejected.
