FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us

How long does verification take?

Typically within 24 hours. Our admin team verifies your bank transfer and activates your brand once confirmed.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes! Request a plan change anytime. Submit a new payment and our admin will switch you immediately.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfers (HBL, Meezan, UBL, and others). Online payment gateway coming soon.

What happens when my subscription expires?

You'll get reminders at 30, 7, and 1 day before. After expiry your portal is read-only until you renew.

Is my data safe?

All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. AWS infrastructure with daily backups. Payment receipts stored in secure, private storage.
