Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

Information We Collect

Name, phone, email, CNIC/NICOP, delivery address, order history, and device information. Required for payment verification per State Bank of Pakistan.

How We Use It

To process orders, payments, deliveries, customer support, and to improve the service. We share information with stores only as needed to fulfill your order.

Data Security

Cards are tokenized via PayFast. Personal data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We never sell your data.
