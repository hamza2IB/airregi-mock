    Refund Policy

    Last updated: July 1, 2026

    All payments are processed as bank transfers and verified manually. Please read our refund policy carefully before submitting a payment.

    1. Payment Method

    RetailOS collects subscription fees via bank transfer only. Payments are manually verified by our admin team within 1–2 business days. Your subscription is activated upon successful verification.

    2. Eligibility for Refunds

    Refunds may be considered under the following circumstances:

    • Duplicate payment — if you accidentally submitted two payments for the same billing cycle.
    • Overpayment — if the transferred amount exceeds the plan price, the excess will be credited to your next cycle or refunded.
    • Service unavailability — if the platform was unavailable for more than 72 consecutive hours due to our fault during a paid period.

    3. Non-Refundable Situations

    • Early cancellation of an active subscription cycle
    • Account suspension due to Terms of Service violations
    • Payments for months already consumed
    • Change of mind after a subscription plan has been activated
    • Rejected payments where the error was on the submitter's side (wrong reference, wrong amount)

    4. Refund Process

    To request a refund:

    1. Email billing@retailos.pk with your business name, payment reference number, and reason for the refund request.
    2. Our billing team will review the request within 3 business days.
    3. Approved refunds are transferred back to your original bank account within 5–7 business days.

    5. Plan Changes

    Switching to a lower plan does not entitle you to a refund for the remaining days on your current plan. The new plan takes effect from the next billing cycle after the plan-change payment is verified.

    For all billing and refund queries, reach us at billing@retailos.pk or call 042-111-RETAIL (Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 6 PM).
